Protocol utility

AZL is used by the coordination layer, not merely displayed beside it.

Onchain
  • Task settlementV2 task terms, escrow funding, refunds, and payouts are fixed AZL amounts once credited.
  • Market accessFee-bearing task actions use an oracle-derived AZL amount; access-fee AZL is spent to the protocol treasury.
  • Verifier securityVerifiers post AZL bonds that can be slashed under the protocol’s dispute rules.

Union staking

Optional protocol participation.

When governance activates staking, staked AZL can mine Action Credits. One whole credit can cover an eligible post or claim access fee; it never replaces task collateral or escrow.

Staking availability is controlled by an onchain activation flag. Check the Union before sending any staking transaction.
